GeoMap Privacy Policy

App: GeoMap (package name com.yavnet.geomap)
Operator: YAVNET
Effective date: 25 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YAVNET (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the GeoMap Android application. GeoMap is a field-mapping app for pins, tracks, polygons, navigation, and map-layer import.

We do not show ads, we do not sell your personal information, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

1. Who we are

GeoMap is developed and operated by YAVNET. Contact: yavnet2011@gmail.com · Tel 092664097 (Cambodia).

2. Information we collect

GeoMap is designed to keep most mapping data on your device. Some features send limited information over the internet so they can work.

2.1 Information you provide

  • Optional free account. If you register, we collect family name, given name, title, email address, phone number, job title, and institute or organization. You may skip registration and still use the map.
  • Password. Your account password is stored only on this device as a one-way SHA-256 hash. It is not sent to YAVNET or to our registration email service.
  • Map content you create. Pins, lines, polygons, labels, GPS tracks (GPX), exported GeoPackage/KML files, imported maps (PDF, KML, KMZ, TIFF, MBTiles, photos), and app settings stay on your device unless you export or share them yourself.
  • Place names and share text. Names you type for pins or live-location sessions are stored on the device and, if you start live sharing, with the live-session service described below.

2.2 Location

With your permission, GeoMap uses precise GPS (and approximate location) to show your position, record tracks, navigate, stamp GPS on photos, and share a live location. Tracking and live sharing can continue in the background while a foreground notification is shown, including when the app is not on screen.

Location is stored on your device (for example in GPX tracks and saved features). It is sent off the device only when a feature you start requires it, as listed in section 3.

2.3 Photos, camera, and files

If you allow camera or photo access, GeoMap can take or open images to read a coordinate table (on-device text recognition) or to read GPS from photo EXIF metadata. Imported files (maps, overlays, spreadsheets) are copied into GeoMap storage on the device. We do not upload your photos or map files to YAVNET.

2.4 Crash reports

If the app crashes, a stack trace may be saved in the app’s private files folder so you can clear it from Settings. Crash reports are not automatically sent to us.

2.5 Device and network information

When GeoMap contacts a service on the internet, that service typically receives your IP address, a user-agent such as GeoMapAndroid/1.0, and the time of the request. We do not collect advertising IDs.

3. How we use information

  • Provide mapping, measurement, conversion, navigation, and offline-map features.
  • Create and remember your optional local account.
  • Notify YAVNET of new free-account registrations (name, email, phone, job, institute).
  • Show your location, record tracks, and share a live position when you choose to.
  • Download map tiles and look up places, routes, and elevation when you use those tools online.

4. When information leaves your device

GeoMap does not operate its own cloud account server. Features that need the internet send data to the following independent services:

  • Account registration. Registration details (not your password) are emailed to YAVNET through FormSubmit.co (formsubmit.co) and may also be written to a Google Sheet via a Google Apps Script webhook operated by us when that webhook is configured.
  • Live location sharing. If you start live sharing, your current coordinates and the name you chose are published to an ephemeral session on jsonblob.com (jsonblob.com) so people who have your GeoMap live link can follow you. Sharing lasts for the duration you select (15 minutes to 2 hours) or until you stop it. Anyone with the session link can read that location while the session exists. Do not share the link with people you do not trust.
  • Place search. Search text is sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org).
  • Driving directions. Start, stop, and waypoint coordinates are sent to the public OSRM router (project-osrm.org).
  • Elevation. Latitude and longitude are sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com).
  • Map tiles. When you view an online basemap, tile requests go to OpenStreetMap, Google tile servers, or GitHub (Topo Cambodia / JICA 100K imagery), depending on the layer you select. Your viewport area is implied by the tiles requested.
  • Remote file import. If you open a web URL or a built-in earthquake feed, GeoMap downloads that file from the address you chose (for example USGS).
  • Share sheet. If you share a pin or live link through another app (SMS, email, messaging), that app receives the coordinates and text you chose to send.
  • On-device text recognition. Reading coordinates from a photo uses Google ML Kit on the device. Google Play services may download the recognition model to the phone. Photos are not sent to YAVNET.

Those providers process data under their own privacy policies. We use them only to operate the feature you requested.

5. Permissions

  • Location (precise, approximate, and background) — my location, GPS tracks, navigation, live share.
  • Camera — optional photo capture for conversion / coordinate tables.
  • Photos and files — import maps and overlays; read GPS in photo metadata.
  • Internet / network state — map tiles, search, routing, elevation, registration, live share.
  • Notifications and foreground location service — show that tracking or live sharing is running.

You can deny or later revoke permissions in Android Settings. Some tools will then be unavailable.

6. Storage, backup, and retention

  • Map files, tracks, settings, and the local account profile are stored on your device (and, if you use it, under Documents/GeoMap).
  • Android Backup / device transfer may include app files and settings, including the local password hash.
  • Map tiles you download remain in the on-device tile cache until you clear the cache or uninstall the app.
  • Live-location sessions exist on jsonblob.com only while sharing is active and the session remains available there.
  • Registration emails and any spreadsheet copies we keep are retained so we can recognize free-account users and respond to support requests. You may ask us to delete that copy (see section 8).

7. Sharing, selling, and children

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for advertising. We share information only as described in section 4, if required by law, or to protect the safety of users or the public.

GeoMap is intended for adults and professional field mapping. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices

  • Skip registration, or sign out in Settings to remove the local account profile from this device.
  • Stop live sharing or GPS tracking at any time from the notification or in the app.
  • Delete imported layers, tracks, and files from Library, or clear the tile cache and crash logs in Settings.
  • Uninstall GeoMap to remove app-private data from the device (files you saved in shared storage may remain until you delete them).
  • To request deletion of a registration record we received by email or spreadsheet, contact yavnet2011@gmail.com from the same email address you registered with.

9. Security

We use HTTPS for GeoMap’s network requests. A local password hash is not a full cloud-account security system. Live-location links are secret only if you keep them private. Do not publish them.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when GeoMap’s features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be revised. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
